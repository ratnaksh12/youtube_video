# 🎬 YouTube Video Script: ONLY These Movies You NEED to Watch Before Avengers: Doomsday!

**Estimated duration:** 3–4 minutes  
**Style:** Cinematic, suspenseful, fast-paced Marvel storytelling

## [HOOK — 0:00–0:20]

Stop watching every single Marvel movie!

Because if you're planning to watch *Avengers: Doomsday*, you don't need to spend weeks rewatching the entire MCU.

But there are a few movies you absolutely should prioritize.

From the return of legendary heroes to the arrival of Doctor Doom, these movies could make a huge difference in how you experience Marvel's next massive crossover.

So, here are the **6 movies you should watch before Avengers: Doomsday!**

And trust me, the last one is especially important.

## [MOVIE #6 — THUNDERBOLTS*]

Let's start with *Thunderbolts*.

Why? Because the Avengers aren't the only team in the game anymore.

This movie brings together a group of morally complicated characters who eventually become the New Avengers.

And with characters like Yelena Belova, Bucky Barnes, and Sentry entering the bigger picture, this team could be extremely important when the next major threat arrives.

Because when Doctor Doom comes knocking, the world might need a very different kind of hero.

## [MOVIE #5 — DEADPOOL & WOLVERINE]

Next up, *Deadpool & Wolverine*.

And this one is important for one massive reason: the multiverse.

This movie connects Deadpool and Wolverine's world to the larger Marvel multiverse and brings the legacy of Fox's Marvel characters into the conversation.

And here's why that matters.

*Avengers: Doomsday* features legendary X-Men characters, including Professor X, Magneto, and Cyclops.

That means the old Marvel universe could collide with the MCU in a way we've never seen before.

And if you want to understand why these characters matter, this is a great place to start.

## [MOVIE #4 — DOCTOR STRANGE IN THE MULTIVERSE OF MADNESS]

Now things get dangerous.

*Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness* explores alternate universes, incursions, and the devastating consequences of messing with realities beyond your own.

An incursion is when two universes collide in a way that can destroy one or both of them.

Think about that for a second.

What happens when multiple universes begin collapsing, and the most powerful heroes from different realities are forced to face the same enemy?

Could they work together?

Or could they end up destroying each other?

With the multiverse playing such a major role in this saga, this movie gives you some valuable context.

## [MOVIE #3 — AVENGERS: INFINITY WAR]

If you haven't watched *Avengers: Infinity War*, stop everything and watch it.

This is where we see what happens when an enemy is powerful enough to defeat the Avengers at their own game.

Thanos doesn't just threaten the world. He threatens the entire universe.

And the heroes discover that even their combined strength might not be enough.

Why is this important before *Doomsday*?

Because Doctor Doom is another villain whose threat could bring heroes from different corners of Marvel together.

Infinity War reminds us that sometimes, the heroes don't win.

And sometimes, the consequences change everything.

## [MOVIE #2 — AVENGERS: ENDGAME]

Of course, you can't skip *Avengers: Endgame*.

This movie delivers the emotional conclusion to the Infinity Saga and changes the future of the MCU forever.

It gives us the end of Tony Stark's journey, the consequences of Thanos's actions, and a major turning point for the Avengers.

And now, Robert Downey Jr. is returning to Marvel as Doctor Doom.

Yes, the same actor who played Iron Man is stepping into the role of one of Marvel's most dangerous villains.

But remember: **Tony Stark and Victor von Doom are not automatically the same character.**

How Marvel explains this casting choice is one of the biggest mysteries surrounding the movie.

And Endgame makes that mystery even more fascinating.

## [MOVIE #1 — THE FANTASTIC FOUR: FIRST STEPS]

And finally, the movie you should put right at the top of your watchlist: *The Fantastic Four: First Steps*.

Why?

Because Doctor Doom's history in Marvel comics is deeply connected to the Fantastic Four, especially Reed Richards.

Reed is one of Marvel's smartest characters, while Doom combines extraordinary intelligence, advanced technology, political power, and mastery of sorcery.

That makes their rivalry much more than a simple battle between a hero and a villain.

It's a clash between two brilliant minds with very different ideas about power and the future.

This movie introduces Marvel's First Family and establishes their world, making it one of the most relevant films to watch before *Doomsday*.

And with the Fantastic Four entering the larger Marvel story, you definitely don't want to miss their introduction.

## [BONUS — IF YOU HAVE EXTRA TIME]

If you want a better understanding of the multiverse, watch *Spider-Man: No Way Home* as well.

And if you want to understand the bigger story behind the TVA, branching timelines, and He Who Remains, watch *Loki* Seasons 1 and 2.

They're not movies, but they provide useful background for the Multiverse Saga.

## [OUTRO — THE FINAL COUNTDOWN]

So, let's recap your essential movie watchlist:

Number 6 — *Thunderbolts*.

Number 5 — *Deadpool & Wolverine*.

Number 4 — *Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness*.

Number 3 — *Avengers: Infinity War*.

Number 2 — *Avengers: Endgame*.

And number 1 — *The Fantastic Four: First Steps*.

Six movies. One massive Marvel event. And one villain who could change everything.

Of course, this is a recommended shortlist, not an officially confirmed list of movies required to understand *Avengers: Doomsday*.
