## Locations (sets)
- **living** — night apartment (`night`): TV off-right is the key (cool cyan), tower of movie cases, window skyline, warm lamp practical.
- **cinema** — auditorium (`void`): the screen is the key, projector beam, rows of heads.
- **throne** — Doom's castle hall (`storm` + green practicals): gothic windows with lightning, braziers, throne on steps, banners.
- **rooftop** — NYC rooftop (`night`): skyline, water tower, ledge; can turn into a green storm or a cracked sky.
- **cosmos** — multiverse (`void`): nebula, stars, universe bubbles, portals.
- **wasteland** — orange battlefields (`golden`/`dusk`): the Void desert, Titan, the Endgame field.
- **lab / retro** — Fantastic Four retro-future lab and city (`interiorWarm`/`dusk`).
- **tva** — retro orange office, giant clock, timeline monitors (`interiorWarm`).
- **stage** — black stage with spotlights (`void`).
- **blueprint / recap / card** — explanations, the countdown cards and recap board.

## Motifs
- **Green = Doom.** Every chapter ends with green light creeping in.
- **Film case cards** with my own icon art (bolt, katanas+claws, mandala, gauntlet, hourglass, rocket-4) and a gold rank number.
- **Universe bubbles** (spheres with tiny skylines) for the multiverse; cracks for incursions.
- **Freeze-frames:** the cold open ("Stop") and "Think about that for a second".
