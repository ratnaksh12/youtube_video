# 🎬 Is Doctor Doom Secretly Tony Stark? How Endgame Created Doom (Theory)

**Based on:** a fan theory shared on Instagram (credit the original creator in the description)
**Style:** Cinematic, mysterious, emotional

## [COLD OPEN]

Imagine this. The final battle of Avengers: Doomsday. Doctor Doom stands over the fallen heroes, and slowly, he removes his mask.

The Avengers freeze. Because the face staring back at them is Tony Stark.

But Reed Richards and Sue Storm see someone else entirely. They see Victor von Doom.

Same face. Two lives. Two universes.

And according to one fan theory, it all started with a single mistake in Avengers: Endgame.

## [1970]

Remember the Time Heist? Tony Stark and Steve Rogers travel back to 1970, to a military base in New Jersey, to steal the Tesseract.

They get it. But they leave something behind.

Because to everyone living in 1970, the Tesseract simply vanished from a locked vault. And the last person seen near it was Howard Stark.

## [THE RULE]

Endgame gave us one rule for time travel. Changing the past doesn't rewrite your future. It creates a brand new branch of reality.

So in the timeline Tony left behind, S.H.I.E.L.D. has a missing cosmic cube, and only one suspect.

## [THE PLOT HOLE]

Now, you might be thinking: didn't Captain America return every stone at the end of Endgame?

He did. But putting the Tesseract back can't undo what people already saw. The alarms. The questions. The suspicion that now follows Howard everywhere.

## [HOWARD'S CHOICE]

And here's what makes it hurt. In that same scene, Howard talks to Tony about the baby on the way. A father who would do anything for his unborn son.

So when he's forced to choose between a prison cell and disappearing, Howard chooses his family. He takes Maria, leaves America, and starts over in a small country called Latveria.

## [A DIFFERENT FUTURE]

Think about what that changes. Howard's genius never builds American weapons. There is no Stark Industries. Instead, his mind builds Latveria.

And without Stark, the whole world takes a different road. A bright, retro, space age future. The kind of world where four astronauts become the Fantastic Four.

## [VICTOR]

Howard's son is born in that world. But he never becomes Tony Stark.

He becomes Victor von Doom.

Brilliant. Proud. Raised on stories of a stolen cube, and a country that turned its back on his father.

He meets Reed Richards. Sue Storm. Johnny Storm. Ben Grimm. The same kind of bonds the Fantastic Four share in their own world.

But Victor loses everything. And from that loss, Doctor Doom is born.

## [THE DISCOVERY]

Then Doom discovers the truth.

He traces his family's exile back to one moment: the 1970 Tesseract incident. And he finds the man who caused it.

A man with his face. His father's name. His mother's love. Tony Stark.

## [STOLEN LIVES]

Tony was given the life Victor was denied. Howard's love. Pepper Potts. The Avengers. The Stark legacy. A hero's ending.

To Victor, that life wasn't just different. It was stolen.

And that could completely change one of Doom's most chilling lines. In the Doomsday trailer, he tells the heroes they have lived stolen lives, and now they must give them back.

Maybe he isn't only talking about the Fantastic Four. Maybe he's talking about Tony.

## [FRANKLIN]

And remember the end of The Fantastic Four: First Steps? Doom quietly visits Reed and Sue's son, Franklin. In the comics, Franklin can reshape reality itself.

What if Doom doesn't want to rule the multiverse at all? What if he wants to rewrite it, and take back the life he believes was his?

## [OUTRO]

That's what could make Robert Downey Jr.'s casting so meaningful.

When the mask comes off, the Avengers see Tony Stark. Reed and Sue see Victor von Doom.

And stolen lives stops being just a line. It becomes the tragedy that connects the entire story.

Of course, this is only a theory. Marvel hasn't confirmed anything, yet.
