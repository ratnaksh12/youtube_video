# TREATMENT: Reel 02 · CleanYourTV (GitHub Repo of the Day · 01)

**Logline:** a smart TV full of ads is scanned, its ads are switched off one by one, and a clean home screen ignites. It's done by a free GitHub repo, and the link comes by DM.

**Structures considered:** (1) a before → after on one set, with the ads counted down 8 → 0 (chosen: the counter is the hook and the payoff); (2) a three-step tutorial; (3) a "spot the ads" game. (2) teaches but doesn't hook; (3) needs more time than a Reel has.

**Shape:** hook (the ad wall, counting) → the repo (name, #1 trending) → setup (3 steps, rail chips, Wi-Fi pulses) → backup scan → switch-off (each ad locks and collapses like a CRT, the counter drops) → **1.1 s of true silence, screen black** → drop: the TV ignites as a solid hologram with the clean home screen → reversible (a "restored" flash) → which TVs → phone app → **COMMENT "TV"**.

**Camera:** push-out from the ad wall; orbit while the repo is named; a whip to the setup angle (rail on the left); a push-in on the screen for the switch-off; a pull-back crane at the drop; a slow orbit through the facts; settle low under the call to action.

**Sound:** 120 BPM E minor; intro → groove → claps (setup) → sparse (backup) → tense with a riser into the silence → silence → drop at the reveal → outro when "TV" locks. Each ad switch-off is a down-blip in key.

**Look:** the account's home look (Hologram HUD, violet lines, lime only on locked values). The TV's screen is opaque, so the set's back doesn't show through it.
