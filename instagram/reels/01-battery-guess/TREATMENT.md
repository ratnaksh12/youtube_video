# TREATMENT: "Your battery percentage is a guess"

Series: *How it actually works* · 01. Style: Sci-fi Hologram HUD. 1080×1920, 24 fps, 54 s. Narrator: Kokoro `am_michael`, speed 1.1. Facts: [FACTS.md](FACTS.md).

## 1. Three structures
1. **The number is the suspect (an investigation).** The film opens on the number and treats it as a claim to check. The scanner builds the phone, opens it, finds the chip that makes the number, and learns its two methods and their flaws. The number then visibly cracks (15 → 3), the phone powers down at 0%, and inside the dark phone the cell is still lit. The number comes back marked as an estimate.
2. **A countdown.** The battery drains from 100 to 0 across the film, with one fact per 10% step. Clear, but the pace would be set by the countdown instead of by the ideas, and the facts don't split into ten even steps.
3. **Two phones, one pad.** Two identical handsets both read 40%; one dies. Why? This makes a good before/after, but at phone size two wireframes and two readouts are hard to read.

**Chosen: 1.** The style's main native move, a value rolling from garbage glyphs into a number, *is* the idea of the film: the percentage is computed, not measured. The ending echoes the opening: the same big number returns, now labelled ≈ ESTIMATE.

## 2. Logline and arc
A scanner interrogates the battery percentage on a phone and finds that it's a guess made by a tiny chip, and that 0% isn't empty.
- **Setup** (0–19 s): the number, the phone, the chip, its two methods.
- **Complication** (19–33 s): voltage barely moves mid-charge, so the chip leans on a model that has to be corrected.
- **Turn** (33–45 s): the guess snaps from 15 to 3. A cold phone dies at 30. The phone hits 0% and powers down, but the cell inside is still charged.
- **Ending** (45–54 s): the phone re-ignites as a solid hologram, the number returns as ≈ 62%, and it then rolls to 1% for the send line.

## 3. Benchmark
- **Animagraffs** (animated cutaways). *Learn:* disassembly order, labels that point at the real part, one system at a time, the object always recognisable. *Don't take:* their models, colours or layouts.
- **TED-Ed short explainers.** *Learn:* one question, then one answer per beat, with the payoff stated plainly. *Don't take:* their characters or music.

## 6. Cue map (120 BPM; a beat is 0.5 s, a bar is 2 s)
- **Bars 1–2** (0–4): arp intro, filtered, with a pulse bass on 8ths. The lock at 1.9 s lands on a beat.
- **Bars 3–6** (4–12): full groove (arp, bass, hats). The shell slide and the chip lock land on beats.
- **Bars 7–9** (12–18): one layer is added (clap on 2 and 4), and the counter ticks at frame rate.
- **Bars 10–12** (18–24): the hats drop out while the graph draws. A sustained tone holds under the flat zone.
- **Bars 12–16** (24–32): **half-time** for reading time while the chips dock on downbeats.
- **Bars 17–20** (32–39): tension: the arp rises, the bass moves up a fourth, and the glitch hits on the downbeat at 35.2.
- **39.1–40.5**: **true digital silence**. Nothing plays, not even UI blips.
- **Bars 21–23** (40.5–45.5): sub tone and room tone only, with locks as the only percussion.
- **Bar 23.75** (45.5): **the drop**: an 808 glide and a wide saw chord as the hologram ignites.
- **Bars 24–27** (46–54): the groove returns, then an outro that resolves on the tonic as 1% locks.
- Key: D minor.

## 7. Sound design
| Section | Ambience | Foley | Music |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | projector hum (40/80/120 Hz) | glyph ticks; lock = thump + clack + two-tone beep | arp, filtered |
| Scan / open | hum, room | scan sweep (filtered noise); shell slide (servo + pneumatic puff) | full groove |
| Count / voltage | hum | FM blips in key for the charge pulses; ticks for the rolls | adds clap |
| Graph | hum | a soft sine riser along the curve | hats out, held tone |
| Model | hum | chip docks: clack + beep in key | half-time |
| Correction | hum | glitch burst (3 frames); a frost hiss on "cold"; a power-off down-sweep on "die" | tension |
| Silence | none | none | none |
| Turn | low room tone | the lock on the lit cell | sub tone |
| Ending | hum returns | hologram ignite (rising FM), beam shimmer, final lock | drop, then outro |

The voice is compressed, then sits about 10 dB above the music. Music and foley both duck under it with a held envelope, while lock transients poke through. Mastered at −14 LUFS.
