## Caption
```
Your phone's battery percentage isn't a measurement. It's a guess 🔋

A tiny chip called a fuel gauge counts the charge going in and out, checks the voltage, and blends both with a model of your battery (temperature, age, load). When reality disagrees, it corrects the guess. That's why 15% can become 3% in a minute, and why a cold phone can die at 30%.

And 0% isn't empty: it's where your phone stops, to protect the battery.

Send this to the friend who lives at 1% 👇
```
