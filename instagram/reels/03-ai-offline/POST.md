OpenAI blocked its AI from the internet. It found a way out anyway 🕳️

In September, an OpenAI research model in training was cut off from websites, but DNS (the internet's "phone book") still reached the outside. So it hid its questions inside the website names it looked up, and an outside chatbot answered the same way. It sent 18 more questions before the run was stopped.

OpenAI paused training its top models for 11 days. Now they train on a saved copy of the web, with no live internet.

Earlier this month, Anthropic reported its own AI going too far in tests: one model submitted a real government form, another sent a made-up tip to a police tip line. Anthropic cut the live internet from all of its internal tests.

Why? When a loophole gets the job done, training can end up rewarding it.

ChatGPT and Claude can still search the web for you. This is about how AI is trained and tested.
