# TREATMENT: Reel 04 · How ChatGPT writes a sentence (How It Actually Works · 02)

The first reel in the **Pixel Room** look (see `instagram/lab/pixel-room/README.md`). Its grammar is modelled on a reference reel: a lit pixel diorama, a mascot, kinetic captions instead of subtitles, and something new every 1–2 s.

**Logline:** BIT, our robot, watches a sentence get written one token at a time: chopped into tokens, scored, picked, repeated. Then it learns why the same question gets different answers.

**Shape:**
1. **Hook:** "ChatGPT doesn't KNOW what it's going to say." BIT's face shows "? ?".
2. **Into the monitor:** a tokenizer splits a sentence into coloured chips, "1 token ≈ ¾ of a word".
3. **"Every token it knows":** a cloud of tiny cubes streams out of the monitor and swirls, then gathers into a glowing "?" slot.
4. **"The cat sat on the…":** word blocks drop in; three bars rise (mat 60 %, floor 10 %, moon ≈ 0 %); BIT points; "mat" flies into the sentence.
5. **The loop:** a high shot; a token lands every 0.36 s and the counter runs to #400 ("hundreds of times").
6. **"NOBODY tells you":** a close-up on BIT; the same start, but "floor" (10 %) is picked and "mat" turns red.
7. **Temperature:** a thermometer fills and the bars flatten and wobble. "…so it doesn't sound like a ROBOT": BIT looks unimpressed.
8. **Regenerate:** the last word flips (mat / floor / rug / sofa), "↻ answer n".
9. **Call to action:** a comment card, "Comment TOKEN", typed into a box, "→ a free tokenizer tool, in your DMs".

**Camera:** a slow push on the room; a swing into the monitor; a pull back with the token cloud; a near-static stage with a drift; a rising high shot for the loop; a dive to BIT; back to the stage; settle wide for the call to action.

**Sound:** 120 BPM E minor. Intro → groove (monitor) → claps (the sentence) → tense with a riser (the loop) → turn, no rhythm ("nobody tells you") → drop as it doesn't pick the top one → groove → outro on the call to action. Effects from the kit library: pops in key for each word, roll ticks for the loop, whooshes on every flight, a red-flag warning on the rejected "mat", blips as temperature rises.
