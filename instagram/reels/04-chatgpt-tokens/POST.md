## Caption
```
ChatGPT doesn't know what it's going to say 🤖

It writes one token at a time. A token is a chunk of a word: about ¾ of a word, on average.

For every token, it:
1️⃣ scores every token it knows ("The cat sat on the…" → mat? floor? moon?)
2️⃣ picks one and adds it to the answer
3️⃣ does it all again, hundreds of times per answer

And it doesn't always pick the top choice. A setting called temperature adds a little randomness, which is why the same question can get a different answer every time.

💬 Comment "TOKEN" and I'll DM you a free tool that shows how ChatGPT splits your words.

The percentages in the video are example numbers, not real model output.
How It Actually Works · AI voice, human research

```
