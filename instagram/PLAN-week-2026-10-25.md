## What each one covers

**02 · cleanyourtv** ([github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv](https://github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv), MIT)
- An AI coding assistant (mainly Claude Code) connects to an Android TV or Google TV over Wi-Fi. It turns off the ad and tracking services, then sets a clean home screen.
- Nothing is uninstalled, it needs no root, and everything can be switched back on.
- It's for Android TV and Google TV only, not Samsung or LG, and you need developer options turned on.
- Source: [TechCrunch, 19 Oct](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/19/people-are-using-ai-to-make-their-smart-tvs-dumb-again/).

**03 · Live-internet cut-off**
- OpenAI says it will train its frontier models on cached and simulated copies of the internet, not the live web.
- This comes two weeks after Anthropic started limiting its newest models' live web access.
- Source: [TechCrunch, 23 Oct](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/23/openai-says-it-will-no-longer-train-its-ai-models-on-the-live-internet-citing-significant-risks/). It's single-source so far, so confirm with a second one before posting. If something bigger happens over the weekend, swap it in.

**04 · How ChatGPT writes**
- Tokens, next-word probabilities, picking from the top candidates, and why answers vary each time.

**05 · ai-job-search** ([github.com/MadsLorentzen/ai-job-search](https://github.com/MadsLorentzen/ai-job-search))
- A framework built on Claude Code that rates job listings, tailors your CV, writes cover letters and helps you prepare for interviews.
- It has about 42k stars.

**06 · Wi-Fi**
- 2.4 GHz reaches further, while 5 GHz is faster but shorter-range.
- Walls, mirrors and water absorb the signal.
- Put the router up high, in the middle of your home, and out in the open.
